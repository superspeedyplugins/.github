## Why Super Speedy?

- Built and tested for large WordPress and WooCommerce databases.
- SQL-first engineering instead of relying solely on caching.
- Designed for catalogues containing hundreds of thousands or millions of records.
- Performance improvements for visitors, administrators and background workflows.
- Documentation, Discord help and commercial support from the people building the plugins.
